# Copy Deck (Stage 4, guide p.12)

**Voice:** clear, warm, practical, restrained. No clinical promises, invented reviews, efficacy claims or unsupported ingredient claims. No em dashes.
**Facts allowed:** only copywriting/product_facts.md (checked 2026-10-12).
**Label on every page:** "Independent portfolio concept. Not affiliated with Sephora. No real purchases."
**Status key:** Draft = AI-drafted, waiting for Disha's choice. Final = Disha chose and edited. Nothing is called a "winner"; A/B choices are judgment, not test results.

Each asset: brief, Draft A, Draft B, Claude's recommendation, **Disha's choice (blank until decided)**.

## LP-01 Landing page hero
**Brief:** Audience: first-time skincare shoppers. Intent: compare options. Funnel: consideration. Action: explore the 3 products. Evidence: audience hypothesis; 3-star reviewers decide on scent and price.
| | Headline | Supporting line | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Find a clearer starting point for your routine | Compare one cleanser and two moisturizers, with scent, texture and price in one place. | Explore the products |
| **B** | Two steps. Fewer guesses. | See how each product feels, smells and fits your skin type before you choose. | Compare the three products |
**Claude recommends:** B. Shorter, and "fewer guesses" speaks to the 3-star reviewers who were unsure. A is safer and closer to the guide's starter copy.
**Disha's choice (FINAL, 2026-10-12):** Combined. Headline from B: "Two steps. Fewer guesses." Supporting line from A: "Compare one cleanser and two moisturizers, with scent, texture and price in one place." CTA from B: "Compare the three products".
**Why (Disha):** B's headline is short and memorable; A's supporting line is more specific and names scent and price, the two things undecided (3-star) reviewers cared about most. Not tested; chosen by judgment.

| **B** | **Before you buy:** It has a noticeable scent and doesn't foam much. If either matters to you, start with the 1.6 oz mini. |
**Claude recommends:** A. Honest about the scent without being negative, and keeps the makeup-removal fact.
**Disha's choice (FINAL, 2026-10-12):** A, with the mini size from B: "**What to expect:** A gel cleanser with a cucumber scent and a light lather. It removes everyday makeup, including mascara, per the brand. Prefer no scent? Start with the 1.6 oz mini."
